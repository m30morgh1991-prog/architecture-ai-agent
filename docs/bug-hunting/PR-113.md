## Findings
The strongest research value is in evaluation protocol and architecture patterns: separate wall/room/opening metrics and correction cost; external geometric validation; and vector-first ingestion with provenance, explicit connectivity, and separation of perception from reasoning. The reviewed Revit tools solve different tasks: Drafted generates new narrow-scope schematic house plans, WiseBIM reconstructs existing 2D plans into Revit elements, and Autodesk Assistant/Generative Design supports model/documentation actions or constrained alternatives.
