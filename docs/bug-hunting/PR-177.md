# Bug Hunt Evidence — PR 177 AI Tool Registry and 2D Evaluation Roadmap

## Scope

Documentation-only registry of previously researched architecture-AI candidates and stage-gated evaluation criteria. It does not install tools, add dependencies, import datasets, change runtime behavior, alter PlanModel/ConstraintMap/ApprovedChangePlan, or authorize adoption. The active product scope is 2D plan understanding, generation, editing, alternatives, and professional presentation. Maket AI is distinguished from Maquete AI; 3D/maquette work and pure flattened-image generators are excluded from the active shortlist.

## Bug Hunt

Review focused on scope drift, candidate identity, tool-role accuracy, hardware and offline constraints, licensing/data caveats, presentation editability, geometry preservation, and accidental promotion of research notes into tested capabilities.

## Findings

- The registry explicitly marks prior research as research-noted or candidate status, not a completed hands-on test or benchmark.
- Maket AI is retained as a 2D plan generation/editing/presentation candidate; Maquete AI is excluded and the two names are not conflated.
- The 3D/maquette category is not active. Pure image-generation services and flattened-image-only outputs have been removed from the active presentation shortlist in this revision.
- Presentation remains a high-priority capability: dimension/scale fidelity, legibility, editable annotations, sheet composition, title blocks, print-ready export, and preservation of approved geometry are required evaluation dimensions.
- Candidate tools do not replace the canonical PlanModel, ConstraintMap, ApprovedChangePlan, local regulation sources, or human approval.
- Licenses, dataset terms, offline behavior, API reliance, hardware requirements, privacy, export/editability, and actual availability remain to be checked at the matching project stage.

## Root Cause

Earlier research notes covered tools with materially different outputs and maturity. Without a consolidated, stage-gated registry, image-only tools, 3D services, unverified product claims, or research datasets could be mistaken for practical editable 2D architecture capabilities.

## Regression

- No tool is marked functionally tested or production-ready solely because it appears in the registry.
- Maket AI remains distinct from Maquete AI.
- 3D/maquette and flattened-image-only services are excluded from the active shortlist.
- Presentation candidates must preserve approved geometry and provide reviewable/editable 2D deliverables.
- No candidate output can promote UNKNOWN or NEEDS_REVIEW to PASS.
- No external code, dataset, dependency, credential, or provider is introduced.
- This PR does not alter source-of-truth models or runtime contracts.

## Required fail-closed states

UNKNOWN, SOURCE_REQUIRED, NEEDS_REVIEW, ABSTAIN, and BLOCKED remain unresolved states and never become PASS without evidence. Candidate maturity, licensing, data access, offline capability, hardware fit, pricing, and export rights remain UNKNOWN until checked at the relevant stage.

## Risk Classification

Low-to-medium documentation and product-scope risk. The main risks are scope drift, overclaiming tool maturity, selecting image-only or hardware-heavy services, and confusing presentation imagery with editable geometry. The revised shortlist and explicit stage gates mitigate these risks.

## Negative Tests

- A pure image-generation result must not be accepted as an editable architectural plan.
- A visually attractive output with distorted dimensions or changed locked elements must fail geometry-preservation review.
- A research repository without verified license/data rights must remain SOURCE_REQUIRED / NEEDS_REVIEW.
- A candidate requiring unavailable GPU/API/network capability must not be promoted to a practical test choice.
- Maket AI must not be confused with Maquete AI.
- No candidate may authorize CAD writes, compliance PASS, or a parallel semantic model.

## Unresolved Findings

- Current versions, pricing, licenses, terms, API limits, export rights, and service availability have not been freshly reverified for every candidate.
- Maket AI remains a candidate; no practical acceptance test is claimed.
- Actual low-bandwidth/offline behavior and tablet usability remain NOT_RUN.
- Shared test inputs and architectural ground truth must be validated before comparative scoring.
- No tool integration or production-readiness decision is made by this PR.

## Final Decision

Documentation and research-roadmap update only. Eligible for human review after all exact-head CI gates complete successfully. No tool adoption, installation, runtime integration, 3D work, or H-stage advancement is authorized.
