# Bug Hunt Evidence — PR 167 Architecture Regulation Reference Pack

## Scope

This PR adds a documentation-only reference pack, a JSON rule-candidate registry, an implementation/test plan, and an initial map of Shiraz building authorities and regulation-acquisition channels. It does not add executable compliance rules, modify the runtime rule engine, or grant editing or approval permissions.

## Bug Hunt

Review focused on false legal authority, stale editions, jurisdiction mismatch, missing source traceability, accidental promotion of user-supplied dimensions into enforceable rules, and confusion between municipal, Article 5, Nezam Engineering, national technical regulations, and case-specific permit decisions.

The registry was parsed as JSON during review. It contains 25 unique rule IDs, all marked CANDIDATE, and has no missing required registry fields. The source list contains four source records. The rule records currently point to AR-SRC-003 (the user's supplemental summary); the uploaded PDFs are listed as references but have not been independently re-extracted into page-level rule evidence by this PR.

## Findings

- The registry is explicitly REFERENCE_CANDIDATES and the rules are CANDIDATE, not enforceable compliance logic.
- The two user-provided PDFs are recorded as unverified source references; their binary contents are not committed by this PR.
- The Shiraz revised detailed-plan PDF (AR-SRC-004) could not be retrieved in the recorded attempt. Its contents remain NOT_INGESTED / SOURCE_REQUIRED.
- Article 100 and related court decisions are recorded with source-quality caveats; primary official Gazette/judiciary copies remain pending for several entries.
- The authority map distinguishes municipal planning, Article 5 approvals, Fars and national Nezam Engineering channels, National Building Regulations, fire, heritage, council and utility roles. This is a discovery map, not a definitive legal delegation opinion.
- The registry currently has 25 unique rule IDs, no duplicate IDs, and no missing core fields (rule_id, source_id, category, status, severity, inputs, predicate).
- No executable evaluator, runtime dependency, PlanModel change, CAD mutation, or production compliance claim is introduced.

## Reproduction

Parse docs/reference/architecture-rule-registry.json as JSON. Enumerate rules and verify unique rule_id values, non-empty source_id/category/status/severity/predicate, and an inputs array. Inspect source AR-SRC-004 and Article 100-related entries: their verification values must remain visibly unverified/pending where primary material has not been obtained. Inspect docs/reference/architecture-rules-engine-plan.md to confirm the document describes future implementation/testing only and is not runtime logic.

## Root Cause

The project needs a traceable place to preserve user-supplied architectural criteria and map the authorities that publish or approve building requirements in Shiraz. Without explicit source-quality and applicability fields, future code could mistake a summary, an outdated PDF, a secondary legal reproduction, or a news report for a current binding rule.

## Regression

The registry's JSON syntax and structural inventory were checked. This is a documentation and data-shape review only. Runtime rule evaluation, legal source verification, current-edition confirmation, and municipality-specific compliance tests are not claimed by this PR. Any later executable rules must have separate deterministic tests for units, finite numeric inputs, applicability, missing context, conflicting sources, and source-bound evidence.

## Required fail-closed states

- UNKNOWN: applicability or meaning cannot be determined from available evidence.
- NEEDS_REVIEW: a human must reconcile jurisdiction, edition, project type, source conflict, or legal interpretation.
- BLOCKED: malformed registry data or a prohibited unsupported promotion prevents evaluation.
- SOURCE_REQUIRED: the authoritative source or exact page/section has not been acquired.
- PASS: prohibited for official compliance when the source, edition, jurisdiction, applicability, or measured evidence is unverified.
- No reference candidate may silently become a binding rule or authorize editing.
