## Root Cause

Regulatory research often arrives through secondary reproductions, press reports, incomplete document copies, or records with inconsistent dates and jurisdiction. Without explicit provenance and uncertainty, a downstream rule engine could mistake a research lead for a current binding requirement or apply a case-specific legal holding too broadly.

## Regression

- Registry JSON syntax and structural consistency are validated by the PR checks.
- Source IDs and candidate rule IDs must remain unique.
- Missing official documents, hashes, maps, applicability, or exact effective dates remain SOURCE_REQUIRED / NEEDS_REVIEW.
- A press report cannot authorize a legal threshold.
- A secondary reproduction cannot be described as an independently verified official publication.
- A legal holding cannot be generalized beyond its stated case and scope.
- Documentation-only changes do not alter runtime, geometry, PlanModel, ConstraintMap, or editing behavior.

## Required fail-closed states

- UNKNOWN: legal meaning, date, applicability, or jurisdiction cannot be determined from verified evidence.
- SOURCE_REQUIRED: the official primary document, map, publication copy, or hash has not been obtained.
- NEEDS_REVIEW: legal scope, jurisdiction, date, or conflict requires human reconciliation.
- BLOCKED: malformed registry data or unsupported promotion prevents downstream evaluation.
- PASS: prohibited for official compliance while source, edition, jurisdiction, applicability, or measured evidence is unverified.
- No candidate source or rule may silently become binding or authorize editing.
