# Bug Hunt Evidence — PR 178 Integrated Shiraz/Fars Regulatory Watch

## Scope

Integrate the 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-21 regulatory research branches without changing runtime behavior or legal authority. Reconcile source identifiers, preserve provenance, keep candidate rules non-executable, and correct the Topic 24 tall-building threshold conflict with explicit uncertainty.

## Bug Hunt

Review focused on colliding source IDs, dangling rule references, duplicated sources, source provenance, primary-versus-secondary evidence, report-to-registry references, M24 threshold attribution, jurisdiction/date context, and accidental compliance PASS.

## Findings

- PR #170 and PR #175 used AR-SRC-010–015 for different documents. Their independent merge would silently corrupt source identity.
- The 2026-11-19 source identities and 31 candidate rules are preserved. New unique 2026-11-21 sources are assigned AR-SRC-022–025.
- Duplicate M24 and MRUD 350 cm source records are reconciled into existing AR-SRC-013 and AR-SRC-019 with additional provenance notes, rather than duplicated.
- The 2026-11-21 report references are remapped to the reconciled source IDs.
- Every rule source_id resolves to a source record; source IDs and rule IDs are unique.
- The old 27 m / 23 m tall-building attribution is corrected to the reproduced M24 47 m / 44 m text, while the conflict and official-source gap remain explicit.
- M19 transition, M12 fifth edition, and judgment 1405313900001131406 remain reference-only; no new executable rules are added.
- The 350 cm Shiraz height lead remains non-binding and cannot produce a legal threshold or compliance result.
- No runtime, PlanModel, ConstraintMap, geometry, CAD write permission, or compliance evaluator is changed.

## Reproduction

1. Parse `docs/reference/architecture-rule-registry.json` as JSON.
2. Assert source IDs are unique and rule IDs are unique.
3. Assert every rule `source_id` exists in the source registry.
4. Verify sources AR-SRC-022–025 correspond respectively to Topic 19 fifth edition, Topic 19 transition extension, Topic 12 fifth edition, and judgment 1405313900001131406.
5. Verify AR-SRC-013 preserves both M24 source/provenance caveats and AR-SRC-019 preserves the MRUD source plus the 2026-11-21 timeout/press-lead note.
6. Verify the 2026-11-21 report uses AR-SRC-013, AR-SRC-019, and AR-SRC-022–025 consistently.
7. Verify M24 47/44 m values are marked reproduced/source-gated and 27/23 m is retained only as a conflict, not attributed as the M24 threshold.
8. Confirm no executable rules, runtime modules, or CAD permissions changed.

## Root Cause

The two source PRs were independently created from the same main SHA and allocated the same sequential source IDs to different regulatory documents. PR #175 also carried a shorter registry snapshot, so merging it after PR #170 could overwrite the larger registry and lose source records/rules. The documentation files also diverged because both branches edited shared regulation references.

## Regression

- Source IDs are globally unique after integration.
- Rule IDs remain unique and all rule references resolve.
- Existing PR #170 source records AR-SRC-010–021 retain their original meaning.
- New source records use AR-SRC-022–025.
- M24 duplicate source material is consolidated with provenance, not represented as a separate new regulation.
- The MRUD 350 cm news item remains a lead only; the original Article-5 resolution, map, conditions, measurement definition, and effective date remain SOURCE_REQUIRED.
- M19 and M12 notices do not become clause-level executable rules.
- Judgment 1405313900001131406 is not generalized beyond the letter it annulled.
- The old 27/23 m figures are not attributed to the reproduced M24 text.
- No compliance PASS, FAIL, permit prediction, legal authorization, geometry edit, or CAD execution is enabled.

UNKNOWN, SOURCE_REQUIRED, NEEDS_REVIEW, ABSTAIN, and BLOCKED remain unresolved states and never become PASS without authoritative evidence and applicable project context.
