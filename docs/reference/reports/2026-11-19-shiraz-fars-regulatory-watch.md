## Topic 24 — architecture/design implications (reference only)

The retrieved PDF states, among other things:

- **Height definition:** from average sidewalk level beside the parcel to the average roof level (sloped roof) or highest parapet (flat roof).
- **High-rise classification:** at least one of the stated triggers: 27 m or higher; 8 floors or more including ground; or highest usable floor above 23 m.
- Density increases for 8+ floor buildings are restricted to high-rise zones/continuous high-rise axes in the approved development plan; isolated single-building density increase is prohibited by the cited text.
- Height/floor decisions must consider overlooking/privacy, shadowing/daylight/air to neighbors and streets, emergency-collapse access obstruction, access-network width, cityscape, and context massing.
- Residential façade lighting is stated as prohibited; ground-floor/entry lighting for security is required. Visible façade mechanical equipment (except gas-related installations) is stated as prohibited.

These clauses require parcel-level applicability, source-host verification, and reconciliation with the Shiraz detailed plan and other national regulations. The national Topic 24 text does not itself authorize extra floors, height, density, coverage, or land-use changes on a Shiraz parcel.

## Article 100 / 99 — legal update

### Article 100 baseline (text reproduction; primary official link not fetched)

- Permit is required before construction, land division, or urban development within city limits or the city harem.
- Note 1: commission procedure, written defense window of 10 days, decision within one month, and demolition deadline capped at two months when demolition is ordered.
- Notes 2–4: additional floor area / unauthorized building, with different treatment for residential vs. commercial/industrial/administrative zones; fines are not a universal right to retain the violation.
- Note 5: parking deficiency/non-usable parking, where correction is impossible, has a specific commission fine mechanism; the text's 25 m²/space calculation is a statutory text reference, not a design substitute for local parking standards.
- Note 6: encroachment into public streets and other technical/health/urban violations.
- Note 7: supervisor duties to continuously check permit/drawings/calculations and report deviations.
- Note 10: 10-day appeal by municipality/owner or representative to a different Article-100 commission; second commission decision is final in the administrative commission route. Judicial review remains a separate route.
- Note 11: transactional building-value schedule must be adopted and reviewed annually.

**Design vs. enforcement:** Article 100 is a post-violation enforcement process, not a design permission. A paid fine or possible fine must never be interpreted by the design engine as permission to intentionally violate parking, height, use, coverage, or permit requirements.

### Clause 3 of Ministry of Interior circular 239024 (1402/11/14)

Judgment No. 1403313900001829587 dated 1403/08/01 **annulled clause 3 from its date of adoption**. The clause's categorical rule requiring demolition of unroofed construction was invalidated. The holding is limited to that clause; it does not invalidate the entire circular or create a blanket right to fine every unfinished/unroofed structure. Official Gazette copy could not be retrieved (the linked Qavanin PDF returned 404).

### Judgment 646865 dated 1404/03/13

The reproduced full text applies the Article-55(24) mechanism to commercial operation in a non-commercial area contrary to the permit: commission 100 can order closure after the statutory process, not a fine or demolition for that use alone. It overturns the 2012 General Board precedent only to the stated extent. It expressly distinguishes professional offices listed in the statutory provision (e.g., lawyer/medical/engineering office) from commercial use. The exact facts and statutory wording must be checked before application.

### Judgment 1295686 dated 1404/05/28 — change of use

The General Board's later judgment clarifies:
- Article-100 fine provisions for notes 2–4 are tied to construction/extra area, not a standalone operating-stage change of use.
- Change of use in a non-commercial area contrary to permit is handled by the Article-55(24) closure mechanism; the ruling says not to impose an Article-100 fine for that use-change.
- Urban land-use change falls within Article-5 commission jurisdiction; Article-100 commission cannot legalize an unauthorized use.
- The judgment is binding for similar cases under Article 89.

This is **not** a blanket exemption for construction performed to create a new commercial building or extra area. Those construction facts must be separated from the use-change fact.

### Advisory opinions 7/1405/261 and 7/1405/231

- 7/1405/261: execution of a final demolition decision is not ordinarily suspended or sent back merely because execution is difficult or may damage other parts; exceptions require the stated judicial remedies. The municipality may not enlarge the demolition area beyond the decision, and technical expert review of the execution method is required to avoid damage outside the violation.
- 7/1405/231: Article 100 does not decide or validate unlawful land subdivision; Article 101 and other legally competent authorities address subdivision. Article 100 may deal only with construction operations independently performed without permit.

Both are **non-binding advisory opinions** and remain secondary-source leads until official copies are obtained.

### Article 99 boundary

The distinction is geographic and legal, not simply “rural vs. urban”:

- City limits / approved city harem: Article 100's permit and commission framework applies (subject to the exact parcel and legal status).
- Outside the approved city harem: the Article-99 special commission is the relevant route for unauthorized construction, with a distinct statutory basis.
- Verify the official city-limit/harem map and the date the violation occurred. A later boundary/harem change may affect jurisdiction. Do not infer jurisdiction from a postal address, satellite image, or current service map alone.

The 1403/05/09 judgment cited above is a caution against automatically importing Article-100's technical/urban/health demolition grounds into Article-99 proceedings.

## Addendum — additional Article-99 findings (2026-11-19)

Two more sources were retrieved and read in full-text reproduction:

### Judgment 1403313900001065343 — 1403/05/09
This judgment annulled only the specified clauses of Ministry of Interior circular 145016 dated 1401/07/19:
- Clauses 7 and 8: forced demolition deadline/execution procedure.
- Clause 9: 50–70% fine formula.
- Clause 10: re-referral for demolition if the fine is unpaid.
- Clause 14: allocation/expense of collected construction violation fees and fines.

Do not extend this judgment to the entire Article-99 guideline or the statutory commission. Official publication copy remains pending.

### Advisory opinion 7/1403/747 — 1403/10/30
The opinion states the **date of the construction violation**, not the later/current parcel boundary status, controls jurisdiction when a parcel was outside the city's harem at the time and later became included. In that scenario, Article-99 commission retains jurisdiction for the earlier violation; a violation occurring after entry into the city limits/harem is Article-100.

This is a non-binding advisory opinion, but it is a high-value routing safeguard: preserve violation date, historical boundary/harem evidence, and current boundary separately in the data model. The original signed opinion is still required.

## Addendum — Article 99 circular annulment and violation-date jurisdiction (2026-11-19)

### Judgment 1403313900001065343 (1403/05/09)
The full reproduced text confirms that only these clauses of Ministry of Interior circular 145016 dated 1401/07/19 were annulled in the specified respects:
- Clauses 7 and 8: mandatory demolition and its execution/deadline.
- Clause 9: the 50–70% fine formula.
- Clause 10: re-referral for demolition after non-payment.
- Clause 14: earmarking/expense of collected fees and fines.

This does not annul the entire Article-99 procedure or the whole guideline. Official primary copy is still pending. Source: [full-text reproduction](https://amagestate.com/legal/article-99-building-demolition-ruling/).

### Advisory opinion 7/1403/747 (1403/10/30)
Jurisdiction for the described boundary-change case is based on the location status **at the time of the violation**: if the parcel was outside city harem then and later enters, the earlier violation remains with Article-99 commission; a violation after entering city limits/harem is Article-100. This is a non-binding advisory opinion, but it is an important data-model safeguard. Source: [opinion 7/1403/747](https://hoshyarvakil.ir/opinion/7-1403-747/).

Therefore, a rule must retain the violation date and historic city-limit/harem map; current parcel status alone is insufficient.
